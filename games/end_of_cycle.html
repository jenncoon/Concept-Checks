Welcome, Timekeeper!

Every financial statement tells the story of a business. For that story to be accurate, every transaction must be recorded in the right chapter, at the right time. A company that records revenue too early, or forgets to record an expense it has already incurred, is not telling an accurate story. Investors, lenders, and managers rely on these numbers to make real decisions. Your job in this concept check is to make sure the story reflects reality, not just what is convenient.
