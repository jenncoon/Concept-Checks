Before your score is revealed

Reflect on the Experience

Take a moment before seeing your score. Honest reflection matters more than the number.

You have just decoded Maya’s story from first transaction to balanced ledger. Now take a few minutes to connect that experience to your own understanding.
Reflection 1 of 2

Think about a transaction from your own life this week. Maybe you bought coffee, paid a bill, or received a paycheck. Describe what happened using debit and credit language. Which accounts were affected, which side did each go on, and why?

Be specific. There is no wrong answer here.
Reflection 2 of 2

Look back at Maya’s story. Pick one specific moment where something changed in her books and explain why the accounting happened at that moment and not earlier or later. What does that timing tell you about how accrual accounting works?

Connect Maya's story to the accrual concept. Your own words matter more than textbook language.
